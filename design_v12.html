Frozen standard

The outcome definition and T+0 / T+7 / T+30 horizons are deliberately absent from controls. The scoring model is a hand-written v0 heuristic, not fitted weights; sources remain Luma calendars, Haas CampusGroups, and Partiful—not every event in the Bay Area.

The product thesis

A calendar is not the point.

Skip or Go is a personal decision system: watch people and rooms with real possibility, not every event that arrives.

Permission to say no

Most listings are not decisions.

Most event tools optimize for discovery. This one makes a deliberate week possible: a room can be interesting and still not be the right professional plan. A settled SKIP is a result, not a missed opportunity.

What P means

P(contact ∪ build) is a pre-event bet.

It means: before you go, how likely is this event to lead either to a meaningful contact after the room or to a traceable build that begins because of it? It is not a measure of whether the night is fun, prestigious, or socially valuable. Those things can matter, but they do not turn an event into a professional hit.

Can you participate?A workshop or build night gives you something to do; a talk can be relevant but still be spectator.
Who is in the room?Target-company proximity matters more than prestige or seniority alone.
Is there a warm way in?A prior hook, a companion, or a recurring context can make an interaction more likely to continue.
What is the friction?Room size, cohort saturation, travel, and timing shape whether the opportunity is practical.

These are the major inputs, not a formula. Every professional event can be opened to inspect its own evidence. The result is a plain GO, PART, SKIP, or conditional GO IF.

A transparent v0, not a magic recommendation

Scoring picks the room; follow-up is what converts it.

Each score is a stated probability of contact or build, not an achievement metric. Professional events are evaluated from format, whether you can participate, proximity to the people you want to meet, prior hooks, cohort saturation, group context, room size, and travel cost. Social and cohort plans remain visible because belonging matters, but they keep their own lane.

Read-back is the hinge

What happened has to be attached to the event.

Without an outcome record, the score is only taste. Read-back records what actually happened at T+0, T+7, and T+30; it separates facts that can be corrected now from patterns that must earn repeated evidence. First choose the event, then add your own words.

What the system sees—and what it does not.

The system sees followed Luma calendars, Haas CampusGroups, and Partiful—not everything in the Bay Area. Unknown data remains unknown, and suppressed events are counted rather than silently dropped. Source provenance, unknown fields, suppressed reachability, and pending hypotheses stay on the surface.

What remains frozen

The outcome definition and its horizons do not become settings. The system can learn from repeated evidence; it cannot renegotiate what counts as an outcome.
