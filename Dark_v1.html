Vision

Permission to say no

Most event tools optimize for discovery. This one optimizes for a smaller, more deliberate week. It makes the trade-off visible: a room can be interesting and still not be worth one of the limited professional slots.

Scoring engine

A transparent v0, not a magic recommendation

Each professional event receives a score from its format, whether you can participate, proximity to the people you want to meet, prior hooks, cohort saturation, group context, size, and travel cost. The score leads to a plain verdict: GO, PART, SKIP, or GO IF. Social plans deliberately sit outside this engine.

The number is a stated bet, not a claim of certainty. Every event can be opened to inspect the evidence behind it.

Why read-back matters
01 / CHOOSEScore the room before going, while the prediction can still be wrong.
02 / ATTENDShow up with a reason, not a vague hope of networking.
03 / READ-BACKRecord what actually happened in your own words, at T+0, T+7, and T+30.
04 / LEARNLet repeated evidence propose changes; a single good night changes nothing.

Read-back is the hinge. Without an outcome record, the score is only taste. Read-back separates facts from inference, protects the calibration record, and lets the system learn from repeated evidence rather than the feeling of one memorable room.
